Image Blurring: Spatial Filter vs. Frequency Domain

CSc 8830 Computer Vision, Homework 3. This homework has two parts:

  1. Implementation - blur an image two independent ways: (a) directly convolving it with a box/Gaussian kernel in the spatial domain, and (b) multiplying its Fourier transform by the kernel's Fourier transform and inverting. Go to Blur: space vs. frequency.
  2. Theory - the convolution theorem says these two operations produce the same result: f * h ↔ F · H. See Theory for the derivation, and the blur page for the numeric proof on a real image (max/mean pixel difference between the two results, which comes out at machine-precision noise).
