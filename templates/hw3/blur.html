On 8-bit images this difference is typically on the order of 1e-10 to 1e-12 in the unclipped float result - i.e. floating-point noise, not a real discrepancy. That is the experimental confirmation of f * h ↔ F · H (see Theory).

Frequency-domain view

The magnitude spectrum (log-scaled, DC at center) of the original image, the blurred image, and the kernel's own frequency response. Blurring removes high-frequency energy (the spectrum shrinks toward the center); the kernel's spectrum shows why - it's a low-pass filter, bright near the center and dark at the edges.
