Human Boundary Segmentation: Classical CV vs. SAM2

CSc 8830 Computer Vision, Homework 4. Three parts, no ML/DL in our own implementation:

  1. RGB boundary - segment a person in a regular color photo using OpenCV's GrabCut (iterative graph-cut / GMM energy minimization on a single image - no trained model or dataset), seeded with a rectangle.
  2. Thermal boundary - segment a person in a thermal (infrared) frame using Otsu global thresholding plus morphology - a person is almost always the hottest (brightest) blob in the frame, so intensity thresholding alone separates them from the background.
  3. SAM2 comparison - both pages let you upload a mask you generated separately with Meta's SAM2 (e.g. from the SAM2 web demo: upload the same image, click the person, export the mask) and compute IoU / Dice against our classical segmentation, plus a visual agreement overlay. SAM2 itself is a deep model, so it's used only as an external reference to score against, not as part of our implementation.

Theory covers how edge detection and region segmentation can be derived in the frequency domain.
