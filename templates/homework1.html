Camera Calibration & Perspective Measurement

CSc 8830 Computer Vision, Module 2. This page ties together the three parts of the assignment:

  1. Calibration - find the camera's intrinsic matrix and distortion coefficients from a set of chessboard photos.
  2. Measurement - use those intrinsics plus a known camera-to-object distance to turn a pixel measurement into a real-world length.
  3. Validation - upload the 20-object experiment results and get the error statistics.

Source code: calibration.py, measure_dimensions.py, validate_measurements.py (see the GitHub repo linked in the report).
